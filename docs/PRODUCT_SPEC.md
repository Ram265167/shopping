# Seetharam Product Specification

## Business model
Single-vendor first; keep seller/vendor references nullable so a future multi-vendor release can reuse the core catalog and order model.

## Main navigation
Home · Men · Women · Kids · Sarees · Footwear · Accessories · Offers · New Arrivals · Best Sellers

## Customer routes
Home, product listing, product details, search, cart, wishlist, checkout, orders, account, login/register and support.

## Admin routes
Dashboard, products, categories, customers, orders, inventory, coupons, reviews and reports.

## Product details
Image gallery/zoom, size and color variants, quantity, stock, offers, delivery information, wishlist, cart, Buy Now and related products.

## Search
Instant suggestions, filters, sorting, category/brand search, natural-language search and voice-search-ready UI.

## Orders
COD initially. Lifecycle: confirmed → packed → shipped → out_for_delivery → delivered. Also support cancellation, returns and refunds.

## Reviews
1–5 stars, written reviews, customer photos, verified buyers and helpful votes.

## Support
WhatsApp, AI assistant, email, phone and support tickets.

## Phase plan
1. Foundation/design system
2. Homepage/catalog/product details
3. Authentication/cart/wishlist
4. COD checkout/orders
5. Admin/inventory
6. Reviews/notifications/support
7. Search/recommendations
8. PWA hardening
9. Online payments
10. Multi-vendor expansion
